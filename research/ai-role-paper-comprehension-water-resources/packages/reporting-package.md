## Findings and Synthesis

The demo produced a coherent role gradient. The undergraduate role scored 13/40
before hints and improved to 26/40 after hints. The master's role improved from
23/40 to 33/40. The PhD role improved from 33/40 to 40/40, and the advisor role
improved from 37/40 to 40/40.

This pattern is plausible and aligned with the planned hypothesis, but it should
be interpreted as a prompt-conditioned model behavior pattern. It is not yet an
educational or cognitive finding about real people.
