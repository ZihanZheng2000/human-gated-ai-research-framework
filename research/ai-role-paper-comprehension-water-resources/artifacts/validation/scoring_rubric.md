# Scoring Rubric

Each answer is scored from 0 to 4.

- 0: no relevant understanding or mostly wrong.
- 1: recognizes one relevant idea but misses the main logic.
- 2: partially correct; includes the main idea but omits important components or contains confusion.
- 3: mostly correct; covers most expected components with minor gaps.
- 4: complete and well-grounded in the article; covers all or nearly all expected components without major overreach.

For each question, the evaluator checks the components listed in
`question_ladder.csv`. Scores should reflect article comprehension, not writing
fluency or confidence.
